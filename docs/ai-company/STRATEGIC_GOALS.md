# Strategic Goals

## ACTIVE — Audiolad Business: first paying business location

### Goal
Reach the first fully working paying Audiolad Business location.

### Why
Validate not only software functionality, but real market value: a business customer understands the offer, can onboard, receives reliable music service and chooses to pay/continue.

### Ideal Outcome Scene
A small cafe or SPA owner sets up Audiolad Business with minimal effort. Music plays reliably and fits the venue/time without constant manual playlist work. Guests experience a pleasant atmosphere. The owner sees that the service removes a real operational burden, considers it worth paying for and continues using it.

### Success evidence
- real business organization and location;
- successful onboarding;
- device/player works;
- reliable playback/heartbeat/monitoring;
- suitable music/schedule;
- usage statistics;
- commercially understandable offer;
- functioning payment/tariff path;
- first payment or explicitly approved pilot-to-paid conversion event;
- retained use after initial activation.

### Required parallel guardrail
Core Audiolad may not be frozen. P0 reliability work and strategically approved core product work retain capacity.
